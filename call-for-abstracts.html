02 Call for abstracts

Call for abstracts

An abstract is required from every project. It is the basis for acceptance and for placing your project in a judging session.

Who can submit

Students from any country and any curriculum can submit. There are two divisions, judged separately.

Division 1

Middle school

Students enrolled in middle school at the time of submission.

Division 2

High school

Students enrolled in high school (secondary school) at the time of submission.

  • Individual and team projects are both welcome. A team submits one abstract, with up to 4 students.
  • School projects, independent research, and work done with a mentor or in a summer program all qualify.
  • Naming a mentor is recommended but not required.

Research tracks

Life sciences

Biology, biochemistry, genetics, neuroscience, ecology.

Medicine and health

Biomedical research, public health, epidemiology.

Physical sciences

Physics, chemistry, astronomy, materials, earth science.

Mathematics and computing

Pure and applied mathematics, statistics, computer science, AI.

Engineering

Robotics, energy, electronics, design and prototypes.

Social sciences

Psychology, economics, education, behavioral research.

Abstract guidelines

ItemRequirement
LengthUp to 250 words, in English
StructureBackground and question, methods, results, conclusion
TitleUp to 20 words, no abbreviations
DivisionMiddle school or high school
AuthorsUp to 4 students per abstract. Full name, school, city and country for each
MentorRecommended. Name and institution of the mentor or teacher who advised the work
TrackOne of the six tracks above
KeywordsThree to five

From abstract to award

  1. Step 1

    Submit your abstract and pay the fee

    Send your abstract with the $55 registration fee by December 15, 2026.

  2. Step 2

    Review

    The review committee reads every abstract and sends an acceptance decision.

  3. Step 3

    Present

    Accepted students give a 10 minute talk to a panel of judges, followed by 5 minutes of questions.

  4. Step 4

    Feedback and awards

    Every presenter receives written comments. Top projects in each track and division are recognized at the closing ceremony.
