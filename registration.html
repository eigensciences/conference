04 Registration

Registration and fee

$55 USD

Registration fee per abstract, paid when you submit.

One fee covers the whole team, up to 4 students. It includes abstract review, your presentation slot, and written feedback from the judges. There are no additional costs after this.

  • The fee is charged per abstract, not per student.
  • Payment is by card through Stripe, a secure online payment service.
  • The fee is non-refundable, including when an abstract is not accepted.
  • Fee waivers are available for students who live in low-income and lower-middle-income countries. The eligible countries are listed below.

Fee waiver: eligible countries
