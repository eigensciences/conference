04 Registration

Registration and fee

$55 USD

Registration fee per abstract, paid when you submit.

One fee covers the whole team, up to 4 students. It includes abstract review, your presentation slot, and written feedback from the judges. There are no additional costs after this.

  • The fee is charged per abstract, not per student.
  • Payment is by card through Stripe, a secure online payment service.
  • The fee is non-refundable, including when an abstract is not accepted.
  • Fee waivers are available for students who live in low-income and lower-middle-income countries. The eligible countries are listed below.

Fee waiver: eligible countries

Students living in the 72 countries below can have the registration fee waived. The list follows the World Bank income classification for July 2026 to June 2027.

How to request a fee waiver

Email us at conference@eigensciences.com before you submit, with the subject line "Fee waiver request". Include:

  • Your name, school and country of residence
  • Your division and the title of your abstract

We will reply with instructions for submitting without payment. Please do not pay the fee if you are requesting a waiver, because the fee is non-refundable.

Submit and pay in one step

Fill in the abstract form. When you click submit, you are taken to the secure Stripe page to pay the $55 fee by card.
