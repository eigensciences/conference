What happens at each stage

  1. Oct 1

    Submissions open

    The abstract form is open. You can submit as soon as your abstract is ready; there is no advantage to waiting for the deadline.

  2. Dec 15

    Abstract and fee deadline

    Your abstract form and the $55 registration fee must both be received by this date. An abstract without a payment, or an approved fee waiver, is not reviewed.

  3. Jan 10

    Acceptance notices

    The review committee emails every team its decision at the contact address given on the form. Check your spam folder if you have not heard by the end of that day.

  4. Jan 15

    Final slides or poster due

    Accepted teams send the final version of their slides or poster so sessions can be scheduled and judges can prepare.

  5. Feb 1

    Conference and presentations

    Each team gives a 10 minute talk to a panel of judges, followed by 5 minutes of questions.

  6. Feb 10

    Awards ceremony

    Winners in each track and division are announced. Certificates and written feedback from the judges are sent to every presenter.

Before the deadline

Checklist

  • Abstract of up to 250 words, in English
  • Title, division and track chosen
  • Names and schools of all authors (up to 4)
  • Mentor details, if you have a mentor
  • A parent or guardian contact
  • $55 fee paid, or a fee waiver approved

Requesting a fee waiver

Waiver requests take time to confirm. If you live in an eligible country, email us well before December 15 so your abstract can still be submitted on time.
