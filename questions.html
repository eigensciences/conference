09 Questions

Common questions

Who can take part?

Middle school and high school students from any country. The two divisions are judged separately.

Do I need finished results to submit?

No. Work in progress is welcome as long as you have a clear question, a method, and at least preliminary findings to discuss.

Can I enter as a team?

Yes. A team of up to 4 students submits one abstract, presents together, and is judged as one project.

What language are abstracts and presentations in?

English.

Does my project need a mentor?

No. Independent projects are judged on the same criteria as mentored ones. Having a mentor is recommended, and if you have one, please enter their name when you submit.

How much does it cost?

The registration fee is $55 USD per abstract, paid when you submit. After that, there are no additional costs.

Does each team member pay?

No. The $55 fee is per abstract, so one payment covers the whole team.

Can I get a refund?

No. The fee is non-refundable, including when an abstract is not accepted.

Is there a fee waiver?

Yes, for students who live in low-income and lower-middle-income countries. The full list of 72 eligible countries is on the Registration page. To request one, email research@eigensciences.com before you submit.

When is the deadline?

Abstracts and the fee are due by December 15, 2026. Acceptance notices are sent January 5 to 10, 2027, and the conference is on February 1, 2027.

How long can my abstract be?

Up to 250 words.

Can I use AI tools such as chatbots?

You may use them for help with language, editing and coding, and you must say which tools you used and how. The ideas, analysis and conclusions must be your own.

How will I know if my abstract is accepted?

The review committee emails the decision directly to the address you submit with, between January 5 and January 10, 2027.

Will I receive a certificate?

Yes. Every presenter receives a certificate of participation, and award winners receive an award certificate.

Can I submit more than one abstract?

Yes. You can submit more than one abstract. The $55 registration fee applies to each abstract.

Can I submit work I have presented at a science fair or another conference?

Yes. Work you have already presented at a science fair or another conference is welcome.

Is the conference in person or online, and what about time zones?

To be announced

Will my abstract be published?

Yes. Accepted abstracts will be published on our website. More information will be shared soon.
