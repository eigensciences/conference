01 About

A research conference built for school students

Most research conferences are designed for people who already hold a degree. This one is for middle school and high school students doing their first serious projects who want an audience that takes the work seriously.

Every participant submits an abstract, presents to a panel of judges, answers questions, and receives written feedback. The strongest projects in each track and division earn awards.

02 Call for abstracts

Call for abstracts

An abstract is required from every project. It is the basis for acceptance and for placing your project in a judging session.

Who can submit

Students from any country and any curriculum can submit. There are two divisions, judged separately.

Division 1

Middle school

Students enrolled in middle school at the time of submission.

Division 2

High school

Students enrolled in high school (secondary school) at the time of submission.

  • Individual and team projects are both welcome. A team submits one abstract, with up to 4 students.
  • School projects, independent research, and work done with a mentor or in a summer program all qualify.
  • Naming a mentor is recommended but not required.

Research tracks

Life sciences

Biology, biochemistry, genetics, neuroscience, ecology.

Medicine and health

Biomedical research, public health, epidemiology.

Physical sciences

Physics, chemistry, astronomy, materials, earth science.

Mathematics and computing

Pure and applied mathematics, statistics, computer science, AI.

Engineering

Robotics, energy, electronics, design and prototypes.

Social sciences

Psychology, economics, education, behavioral research.

08 Policies

Policies

Original work

The research must be the student's own. Copied text or results lead to rejection. Abstracts may be checked for plagiarism.

Credit for help

Name every mentor, teacher or lab that supported the project, and say which parts the student did.

Presenting

At least one student author of each accepted abstract must present to be eligible for awards and a certificate.

Research ethics

Projects involving people or animals must have followed the approval rules of the student's school or institution.

Parent or guardian consent

Participants under 18 need consent from a parent or guardian to register and to appear in photos or recordings.

Use of AI tools

AI tools may help with language, editing and coding, but the research question, analysis and conclusions must be the student's own. Say in your abstract or presentation which AI tools you used and for what. AI tools cannot be listed as authors, and results or references invented by AI lead to rejection. Students are responsible for the accuracy of everything they submit.

Refunds

The registration fee is non-refundable, including when an abstract is not accepted or a student withdraws.

09 Questions

Common questions

Who can take part?

Middle school and high school students from any country. The two divisions are judged separately.

Do I need finished results to submit?

No. Work in progress is welcome as long as you have a clear question, a method, and at least preliminary findings to discuss.

Can I enter as a team?

Yes. A team of up to 4 students submits one abstract, presents together, and is judged as one project.

What language are abstracts and presentations in?

English.

Does my project need a mentor?

No. Independent projects are judged on the same criteria as mentored ones. Having a mentor is recommended, and if you have one, please enter their name when you submit.

How much does it cost?

The registration fee is $55 USD per abstract, paid when you submit. After that, there are no additional costs.

Does each team member pay?

No. The $55 fee is per abstract, so one payment covers the whole team.

Can I get a refund?

No. The fee is non-refundable, including when an abstract is not accepted.

Is there a fee waiver?

Yes, for students from low-income and lower-middle-income countries. Eligible country list and how to apply to be added

When is the deadline?

Abstracts and the fee are due by December 15, 2026.

How long can my abstract be?

Up to 250 words.

Can I use AI tools such as chatbots?

You may use them for help with language, editing and coding, and you must say which tools you used and how. The ideas, analysis and conclusions must be your own.

How will I know if my abstract is accepted?

The review committee emails a decision to the address you submit with. Notification date to be announced

Will I receive a certificate?

Yes. Every presenter receives a certificate of participation, and award winners receive an award certificate.
